### Synthesized Answer

Explainable AI (XAI) refers to the development of techniques and methods that provide insights into the decision-making processes of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) models. The goal of XAI is to make AI more transparent, accountable, and trustworthy by enabling users to understand how AI models arrive at their predictions or decisions.
